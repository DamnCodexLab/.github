# DamnCodexLab

**Team 12 | Digital Circuit Lab (DCLab) | NTUEE, Fall 2026**

Welcome to our GitHub organization!

We are Team 12 from the **Digital Circuit Lab** course at National Taiwan University (NTU).

**DamnCodexLab** is just our playful take on the abbreviation *DCLab*. Don't take the name too seriously :)

## Our Project: Contact-Aware Snake Robot

We are developing a **snake-inspired robot** capable of navigating complex environments, including narrow spaces, obstacles, and uneven terrain.

Our goal is to explore how a snake robot can adapt its locomotion by leveraging whole-body contact sensing and intelligent control.

The project focuses on three key areas:

- **Mechanical Design:** Building a modular snake robot capable of both horizontal and vertical articulation.
- **FPGA-Based Control:** Developing hardware for real-time motor control and sensor processing.
- **Adaptive Locomotion:** Exploring the integration of Central Pattern Generators (CPGs), Reinforcement Learning (RL), and local reflexes to achieve adaptive movement.

Through this project, we aim to bridge digital hardware design, robotics, and learning-based control.

Stay tuned for our progress!
